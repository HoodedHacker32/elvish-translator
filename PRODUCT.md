# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React + Vite (user's choice). Translation engine runs fully client-side; no server, no network calls.

## Users

Tolkien readers and fans who want an English phrase rendered in Elvish — for a tattoo idea, an inscription, a gift, a D&D/roleplay line, or curiosity — and want to know how much of the result is genuinely Tolkien's.

## Product Purpose

Translate English into Sindarin or Quenya, word by word with grammar applied, and show provenance for every word so the user can trust (or question) the result. Success: the output is faithful to Tolkien's attested vocabulary where it exists, honest where it doesn't.

## Positioning

Provenance is the product. Every output word is traceable: attested by Tolkien (with source), a regular derivation from attested forms, or a labeled Neo-Elvish reconstruction. Famous canonical sentences are recognised and returned verbatim from the texts.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Languages: Sindarin and Quenya, toggleable.
- Gap handling: attested Tolkien vocabulary first; well-known Neo-Elvish fill-ins allowed but always labeled non-canon; words with neither are flagged, never invented.
- Grammar: basic rules (articles, plurals, Sindarin lenition after the article, adjective order, pronoun forms) applied deterministically, not by an ML model, to avoid hallucinated forms.
- Offline, local-only.

## Evidence on Hand

No user-supplied assets. Canon phrases must be quoted from Tolkien's published works only; never fabricate attestations or sources.

## Product Principles

1. Never pass invented Elvish off as Tolkien's.
2. Show the working: gloss, source, and grammar step for each word.
3. Canonical phrases beat composed ones.
4. Beauty serves reading — the Elvish line is the hero.
